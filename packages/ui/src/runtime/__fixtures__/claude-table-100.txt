⏺ Two common tools keep terminal sessions alive after you disconnect. Both let you detach and
  reattach, but they differ in features and defaults.

  │ tmux     │ Runs detachable sessions with multiple windows and panes, and offers a highly    │

  ├──────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ GNU      │ Provides detachable sessions and window management on nearly every Unix system,  │

  └──────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Choose tmux for modern workflows and split-pane layouts, and fall back to screen when it is the
  only multiplexer installed on a server.
